Simple Homemade Pancakes

A quick, easy, and fluffy pancake recipe perfect for a cozy breakfast.

>
>

Prep Time

  • Prep time: 10 mins
  • Cook time: 10 mins
  • Servings: 2 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix together the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined (do not overmix!).
  4. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat. Pour batter onto the skillet to form pancakes.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on top, then flip and cook until golden brown on the other side.

Its done, enjoy your meal.
